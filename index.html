Brazil
2 nights

The falls are the largest waterfall system in the world. Day 1: Brazilian side — panoramic elevated catwalks above the cascades. Day 2: Argentine side for the up-close spray, or the Itaipu Dam (the world's second-largest hydroelectric dam). Stay in Foz itself — cheaper than Puerto Iguazú across the border.

Days 14–16
Arica
Chile — City of Eternal Spring
3 nights
→ Bus La Paz → Arica (Chile) — scenic altiplano route via Tambo Quemado border
~5–6h~$15–25/pp

An underrated gem and a perfect decompression from the Bolivian altiplano. Sun year-round, Pacific beaches, world-class seafood. Day 1: El Morro (130m cliff fortress, 360° views), Catedral de San Marcos — designed by Gustave Eiffel. Day 2: Lauca National Park day trip — volcanoes, flamingos, vicuñas, Lake Chungara at 4,500m. Day 3: Azapa Valley, world's oldest mummies (Chinchorro culture, older than Egypt), beach afternoon.

Days 17–18
Santiago
Chile
2 nights
✈ Fly Arica ARI → Santiago SCL — Sky Airline or LATAM, very frequent
30–45m~$40–80/pp

The shortest flight you'll ever take — barely time to eat the peanuts. 2 nights. The main purpose: the Bahá'í Temple of South America, one of only eight continental Bahá'í Houses of Worship in the world, set in the Andes foothills east of the city. Also: Cerro San Cristóbal for panoramic views, Barrio Lastarria for restaurants and bars, Mercado Central for the best ceviche and sea bass of your life.

Four nights means you can actually breathe here. Recoleta Cemetery (one of the world's most extraordinary, Eva Perón is buried here), San Telmo Sunday market, La Boca and Caminito street, Palermo Soho food and nightlife, a proper tango show. The steak will ruin you for all other steak forever. Buenos Aires at Christmas is lively — warm weather, outdoor dining, the city at its summer best.
